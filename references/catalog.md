# Directory seed catalog

Use these as research starting points. Verify each selected destination's current official rules, pricing, eligibility, and submission flow. A catalog entry is neither an endorsement nor permission to publish or buy. Homepage links are deliberate where a stable submission URL is unavailable; follow visible submission links rather than guessing paths.

## Free and mixed options

The following routes were observed during a 2026-10-15 project-submission pilot. These are historical observations; account state, eligibility, publication, and wait times vary. Product Hunt's official guide was also checked on that date. Recheck before each campaign.

| Destination / official starting point | Observed route | Likely audience | Check before submitting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) | Free launch | Technology products and makers | Existing product, maker identity, date/timezone, media. Profile availability and launch-day status may differ. |
| [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/) | Free product profile | Founder-built products | Account, required company facts, directory eligibility. Do not invent revenue or funding. |
| [DevHunt](https://devhunt.org/) | Free queue; paid options | Developer tools | Free launch can be far in the future; verify date before committing. |

## Paid options

Official public pages checked **2026-10-15**. Amounts are advertised USD prices, not checkout quotes. Recheck taxes, cadence, refunds, review rules, and plan immediately before purchase. Promotional claims are not independently verified outcomes.

| Destination / price source | Advertised price observed | Fit and conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Collection](https://thataicollection.com/submit/) | $19 listing; alternative promotion plans advertised from $10 | AI applications. Check excluded categories, checkout plan/cadence and review process. A separate About page contained conflicting free-review language; do not infer a free route or immediate publication from it. |
| [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/submit) | $99 submission | AI tools. Check generated versus owner-supplied copy, delivery/refund conditions and exact checkout. |
| [Startups.fyi official form](https://tally.so/r/3lOGLk) | Featured listings from $29 | Startups and software. Only paid completed submissions enter the queue; publication remains subject to guidelines. Reach the current form through [Startups.fyi](https://www.startups.fyi/) if the link changes. |
| [No Code Founders](https://www.nocodefounders.com/list-your-tool) | $500 lifetime listing | Tools relevant to no-code/low-code founders. Separate newsletter purchase is optional; do not add it automatically. |

**Affiliation:** AI Collection is a maintainer-affiliated directory. Inclusion does not give it automatic priority or waive relevance, verification, or purchase authorization. No affiliate tracking links are used in this catalog.

## Maintaining this catalog

For additions, record an official starting point, audience fit, cost class, source/check date, and meaningful conditions. Label unverified candidates explicitly. Keep dynamic prices here rather than in the main skill. Do not convert traffic claims, domain ratings, or copied directory lists into recommendations without verification. Campaign-specific account problems belong in the private ledger, not as universal site behavior.
