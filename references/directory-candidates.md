# Additional directory research candidates

**Checked October 20, 2026.** 328 additional, deduplicated candidates complement the 22 original entries in [catalog.md](catalog.md): **350 unique research starting points (300+)**.

These are discovery leads, not tested submission workflows. Each row has an observed official starting URL and a dated source. `Homepage observed` means that URL returned a page; it does not establish open submissions, acceptance, free pricing, dofollow links, automation permission or monitoring support. `Access limited` means the site challenged or rate-limited the check. All new cost classes remain **unknown** until official submission terms are inspected.

For a shortlisted directory, verify product fit, existing listings, official submission rules, login/owner identity, actual fees and cadence, queues, badges/backlinks and human-only steps. Follow visible submission links; do not guess routes. An observed Submit link is a research route, not proof that the full workflow was tested. Use the main skill’s private campaign ledger for real work.

Known parked, unrelated, expired and unavailable pages were excluded. Generic search/business directories, editorial awards, developer Q&A sites and integration-development opportunities were excluded from this expansion. The seed entries retain their separate pilot/source notes.

Sources: **R** = [RankInPublic’s public examples](https://rankinpublic.xyz/directory-submission-service#directories); **F** = [Finder Launch’s public directory index](https://finderlaunch.com/directories); **X** = [Grant O’s public backlink list](https://x.com/2whazzuup/status/2112291036315709828). These sources identify candidates only. Their DR, traffic, pricing and link-type claims were not imported as facts. The X article contains 724 mixed leads, including 330 directory rows; only relevant destinations passing this expansion’s scope and availability review were added. The headline totals are not imported as toolkit coverage.

**Affiliation:** AI Collection in the original seed catalog is maintainer-affiliated. No new entry receives priority or a positive recommendation because it appears here.

Machine-readable observations: [directory-candidates.json](directory-candidates.json). Counts: [catalog-summary.json](catalog-summary.json).
